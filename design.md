# Design Direction

## Design Inspiration URL
https://nexus-nmap.vercel.app/

## What We Borrow
Dark developer-console mood, oversized sans-serif typography, monospace metadata, numbered sections, thin borders, modular grids, technical status panels, restrained motion, and compact information panels.

## What We Do Not Copy
No Nexus/Nmap branding, wording, logo, exact layout, graphics, fake logos, testimonials, photos, claims, or statistics. Avoid excessive neon, glow, gradients, glassmorphism, and generic university templates.

## Visual Mood
Modern IT education x developer interface x TVET x JTMK PUO: precise, practical, confident, and premium.

## Layout Rules
Sticky compact navigation, hero with `Explore the Programme`, editorial Introduction/Synopsis, category-based careers, Direction panels, expandable PEO/PLO, semester/track curriculum controls, three pathway cards, leadership/team, CTA, and footer. Keep sections full-width and cards purposeful.

## Color / Contrast Rules
Background `#08090A`, secondary `#101214`, panels `#14171A`, text `#F5F5F5`, muted `#A4A8AD`, Perak yellow `#FFD400`, borders `rgba(255,255,255,.10)`. Use yellow selectively for active states, CTA, section numbers, and `03 TECHNOLOGY TRACKS`.

## Typography Feel
Modern sans-serif for headings/body; readable monospace for codes, labels, metadata, statistics, and status.

## Component Style
Accessible sticky navbar, technical hero panel, editorial content, career explorer, Direction grid, expandable outcomes, technology-data course cards, exactly three SAD/NS/IS track cards, factual leadership/team cards, and guarded external CTA links.

## Mobile Rules
Accessible collapsed menu, stacked layouts, stacked tracks, independently scrollable control rows, course cards instead of wide tables, generous tap targets, no clipping or page overflow, and visible hero CTA/status.

## Accessibility Basics
Semantic landmarks/headings, keyboard buttons/tabs/accordions, visible focus, ARIA selected/expanded states, strong contrast, reduced motion, and honest empty URL handling.

## Anti-Slop Rules
No fake logos/testimonials/stats/lorem ipsum. One primary action. Exactly three PUO tracks: SAD, NS, IS. Do not show the three excluded national tracks as PUO offerings. Do not invent people, content, photos, or URLs.

## Design Verification Checklist
Hero identity and CTA; eight anchors; nested Direction anchors; distinct section treatments; curriculum controls; exactly three tracks; mobile no-overflow; keyboard/focus/reduced-motion checks; honest URLs.
