# Project Brief

## Project Identity
DIT Programme Explorer - a modern Diploma in Information Technology programme website for JTMK, Politeknik Ungku Omar (PUO).

## One-Sentence Concept
A polished responsive one-page site helping prospective students understand the DIT programme, curriculum, three PUO technology tracks, career pathways, and next steps.

## Target User
Prospective students and guardians researching the DIT programme.

## User Goal
Explore official programme information, compare SAD, NS, and IS pathways, and decide whether to enquire or apply.

## Build Shape
Content-led site.

## Shape Confirmation
Confirmed by the learner.

## Version-One Success
A polished one-page site with the official programme-information structure, interactive curriculum and track exploration, career pathways, responsive layout, and `Explore the Programme` as the primary action.

## Now / Later / Never
### Now
One responsive factual programme page, interactive SAD/NS/IS and semester exploration, PEO/PLO and career browsing, leadership/team information, and honest CTA links.

### Later
Additional approved programme pages, richer official resources, and confirmed external destinations.

### Never
Login, accounts, database, backend, payments, live APIs, student portal, admin dashboard, or complex admissions workflow.

## Assumptions
The supplied programme document is the source of truth. PUO offers exactly three tracks in this project: SAD, NS, and IS. Application/enquiry URLs remain placeholders until confirmed.

## Proof Target
Desktop and mobile proof of readable sections, working anchors and interactions, exactly three PUO tracks, accurate programme data, and no broken external links.
